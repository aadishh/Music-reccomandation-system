📷 Webcam & Facial Emotion Processing Notice

Policy Version: {{ camera_consent_version }}

Before activating your webcam, please review and affirmatively approve how your camera frames will be handled.

🎯

Why is webcam access needed?

To capture brief facial frames so our DeepFace AI can infer your emotional expression and recommend music tailored to your mood.

⚡

Strictly In-Memory Server Processing

Frames are sent over encrypted HTTPS and decoded directly in volatile server memory (RAM). They are never saved to disk, SSD, or database.

🛡️

No Biometric Templates or Face Recognition

We do not construct face embeddings, biometric identity vectors, or compare your face against any identification registry. Frames are discarded immediately after emotion scoring.

🔄

Withdraw Consent Anytime

You can revoke camera consent at any time from the Privacy Dashboard. Revoking consent halts camera processing immediately.
